Jack Vitale: Original Musicals



THE SOCIETY (2025)



In 2025, I wrote my first full length original musical, The Society. It was a dystopian rock musical about two brothers, Lorenzo and Carlos, who live in a New York City where everything is being gentrified and under the ownership of a major capitalistic company, who has turned rent into a subscription. There is a large violent gang underneath the train stations, who all bear the masks of the trains of the city, known as the Metromen. Carlos doesn't get involved with the issues/politics of the city, but when he finds out his borther Lorenzo is a part of the Metromen, he makes a mask of his own and goes underground to get Lorenzo to quit the gang. The show has been given several read throughs, and a concept album is in the works. I'm still working on it today, but I learned a lot about writing and performing original theatre, and I am very proud of what I accomplished with The Society.




IMPERIUM (2026)


In July, I was asked by the director of Piper Theatre Productions, a camp I had been attending for 3 years at this point, if I wanted to do a fully staged, but heavily reworked version of The Society next year. I said yes. Over the course of eleven months, I reworked The Society into IMPERIUM, a rock-dystopian contemporary musical. Carlos was genderbent to Carla, and the show, while keeping Lorenzo became much less about the brothers, and more centered on Carla and a new character, Violet, the daughter of the Architect (essentially the president of the world) as they fall in love, and fall apart trying. Very Romeo + Juliet. The show was performed in July of 2026, was a huge success, and I learned a lot about how to rewrite original theatre, music direct, and how to work with a team of real proffesionals (editors, music directors, directors, sound engineers, producers) to make a show come together. I am very proud of what I accomplished with IMPERIUM, and it has motivated me to flesh out and publicly show my work. If you're interested in seeing the show, here's a recording: https://youtu.be/xDdOm1pnSbM?si=ug-ZXJhPZOZ6dIx4




IN PROSPECT (2027)


This one certainly isn't done yet, but I've been working on a new original musical called IN PROSPECT which takes place in well...Prospect Park. It follows eight friends as they slowly fall apart over the course of their last years in high school to their early years of college. But this time, I'm doing something different. I'm making it a movie. Songs, dance, and all that jazz will stay, but this time on camera. We have over one hundred people working on this film, students here, and professionals as well. We have a 2000 dollar budget, and raising the money is hard, but we can do it. We start shooting on 10/3, but I am excited to see where it goes. I have been working on the music and lyrics, and I have a few songs that I am really proud of. The film releases on June 11th, 2027, and I'm very excited to show it to the world.
